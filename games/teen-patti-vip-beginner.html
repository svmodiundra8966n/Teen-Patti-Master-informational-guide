Teen Patti VIP Beginner Guide

VIP is usually not the best place for complete beginners. Most people learn the game on regular Teen Patti Master tables first, then check VIP if they want restricted or higher-entry tables. This guide explains what to know before you try.

What Beginners Should Know

  • Hand rankings are the same as Master and Gold (Trail strongest).
  • Some VIP tables need a higher balance before they open.
  • The number of open tables can be smaller than regular lobbies.
  • Entry requirements can change, so always check inside the app.

If you are still learning the difference between Pure Sequence and Colour, or between blind and seen betting, stay on regular tables for longer. VIP tables do not teach the game differently. They only change who can sit and how large the starting stakes are.

Simple Approach

Learn the six rankings and the basic round structure on standard Teen Patti Master tables. Set a clear limit. Only look at VIP tables after you are comfortable and only if the entry requirements fit the amount you are willing to risk. Stop when you reach your limit.

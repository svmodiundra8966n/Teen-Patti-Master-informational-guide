Teen Patti Gold FAQs

Common questions about Teen Patti Gold answered in simple language. Always confirm live table limits and rules inside the app.

Are the hand rankings different on Gold?

No. Trail is still the strongest, followed by Pure Sequence, Sequence, Colour, Pair and High Card. The order is the same as regular Teen Patti Master.

What makes Teen Patti Gold different from Master?

Mainly higher table limits and larger boot amounts. The core rules and round structure stay the same.

Should beginners play Gold?

Most beginners are better off learning on regular Teen Patti Master tables first. Gold tables cost more when you make a mistake.

Can I lose more money on Gold?

Yes. Because the stakes are higher, the same mistake can cost more chips in a single round.

Is there a separate Gold lobby?

Some versions show a separate higher-stakes lobby. Others simply mark certain tables as Gold. Check inside the app.

Do withdrawal rules change on Gold?

Limits and methods can differ by version. Always confirm the current options inside the app after registration.

Is real-money Gold play legal everywhere in India?

No. Online real-money gaming is restricted under the 2025 Act. Confirm local laws. 18+ only.

Teen Patti Gold Hand Rankings

Teen Patti Gold uses the exact same hand ranking order as regular Teen Patti Master. Higher table limits do not change which hands beat which. Learning the rankings once is enough for both versions.

Ranking Order on Gold Tables

RankHandExample
1Trail / TrioA-A-A
2Pure SequenceK-Q-J suited
3Sequence9-8-7 mixed
4Colour / FlushA-J-6 same suit
5PairQ-Q-5
6High CardA-10-4

Why Rankings Matter More on Gold

Because the stakes are higher on Teen Patti Gold tables, mixing up Pure Sequence with Colour or staying in with a weak Pair costs more chips than it would on a standard Teen Patti Master table. Players who already know the ranking order make fewer expensive mistakes when they move to Gold.

Trail is still the strongest hand. A Pure Sequence still beats a regular Sequence. Colour still beats a Pair. When two hands are the same type, the higher cards decide the winner. Always confirm the ranking shown inside the app after any update.

How to Play Teen Patti Master – Rules & Hand Rankings

Teen Patti Master is a mobile version of the classic Indian three-card game. This page explains the basic rules and hand rankings so you can understand how a round works. It is an independent informational guide only.

Basic Round Structure

Each player receives three cards. A small boot amount is placed in the pot. Players then decide whether to stay blind (without looking at their cards) or look at their cards. After that they can match, raise or fold. The last player standing or the one with the stronger hand wins the pot.

Hand Rankings (Strongest to Weakest)

  • Trail / Trio – Three cards of the same rank (example: A-A-A). Strongest hand.
  • Pure Sequence – Three consecutive cards of the same suit (example: K-Q-J suited).
  • Sequence – Three consecutive cards of mixed suits (example: 9-8-7).
  • Colour / Flush – Three cards of the same suit, not in sequence.
  • Pair – Two cards of the same rank plus one other card.
  • High Card – None of the above. The highest card decides.

Blind vs Seen Play

Blind players usually pay half the amount that seen players pay. This makes early rounds cheaper if you stay blind. Once you look at your cards you become a seen player and must match the higher amount to stay in.

Important Notes

Rules can vary slightly between different builds. Always check the table rules shown inside the app. Real-money features involve financial risk and are restricted under Indian law in many places. Play only if you are 18+ and can afford any loss.

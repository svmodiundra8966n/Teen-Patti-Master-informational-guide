Teen Patti Master FAQs

This page answers common questions about Teen Patti Master. The answers are based on the standard rules of the three-card game and general safety practices. Always confirm live details inside the app.

Game Rules and Rankings

What is the strongest hand in Teen Patti Master?

Trail (three of a kind) is the strongest hand. Three Aces is the highest possible Trail.

Does Pure Sequence beat Sequence?

Yes. A Pure Sequence (suited consecutive cards) ranks higher than a regular Sequence.

What beats a Pair?

Colour, Sequence, Pure Sequence, and Trail all beat a Pair. High Card loses to a Pair.

How many cards does each player get?

Each player receives three cards in a standard Teen Patti Master round.

Blind and Seen Play

What is the difference between blind and seen?

Blind players have not looked at their cards and usually pay less. Seen players have looked and pay more to stay in.

When should I look at my cards?

Many players stay blind while the pot is small and look once the cost rises. There is no single correct moment.

Safety and Legal Questions

Is real-money Teen Patti Master legal in India?

Online real-money gaming is restricted under the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025. Confirm the rules in your location. 18+ only.

How do I install the APK safely?

Download from a known source, allow unknown sources temporarily on Android, install, then turn the setting off again.

Should I share my OTP or UPI PIN?

No. Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, or account passwords with anyone.

General Questions

Are the rankings the same on Gold and VIP?

Yes. Trail remains strongest, followed by Pure Sequence, Sequence, Colour, Pair, and High Card on all three versions.

Can table rules change?

Minor rules and limits can differ between versions or after updates. Always check inside the app.

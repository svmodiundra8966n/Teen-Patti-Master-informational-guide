Teen Patti Master VIP – Private Tables & Higher Entry Guide

Teen Patti Master VIP is aimed at players who want private tables or higher entry levels. The core three-card game and hand rankings stay the same as Master and Gold. The difference is mainly access.

What VIP Usually Means

  • Some builds require a higher balance before VIP tables open.
  • A separate lobby may appear only after certain conditions are met.
  • Table minimums are often higher than regular tables.
  • The number of open VIP tables can be smaller.

Hand Rankings Stay the Same

Trail is still the strongest hand. Pure Sequence ranks next, followed by Sequence, Colour, Pair and High Card. Because the ranking order does not change, the same decisions that matter on regular tables remain important on VIP tables.

Check Inside the App

Entry requirements can differ between versions. After registration, open the app and check whether a VIP lobby is already visible or whether the app asks for a minimum balance first. Do not assume the requirements will stay the same over time.

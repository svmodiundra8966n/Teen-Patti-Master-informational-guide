Teen Patti VIP Hand Rankings

VIP tables follow the same hand ranking order as regular Teen Patti Master and Teen Patti Gold tables. Restricted access does not change which hands beat which.

Ranking Order

RankHandExample
1Trail / TrioA-A-A
2Pure SequenceK-Q-J suited
3Sequence9-8-7 mixed
4Colour / FlushA-J-6 same suit
5PairQ-Q-5
6High CardA-10-4

Why This Matters on VIP Tables

Players who treat Teen Patti VIP as a completely different game often lose chips by ignoring the same ranking rules they already know from standard tables. Trail is still the strongest. A Pure Sequence still beats a regular Sequence. Colour still beats a Pair. When two hands are the same type, the higher cards decide.

Because VIP tables often have higher entry requirements, the cost of a ranking mistake can be larger. Confirm the ranking shown inside the app before you play.

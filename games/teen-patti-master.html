Frequently Asked Questions

What is the strongest hand in Teen Patti?

Trail (three cards of the same rank) is the strongest. Three Aces is the best possible Trail. It beats every other hand type.

Does Pure Sequence beat Sequence?

Yes. A Pure Sequence is three consecutive cards of the same suit and ranks above a regular Sequence of mixed suits.

Should I stay blind or look at my cards?

Staying blind early is usually cheaper because blind bets are lower. Many players look only after the pot grows. Looking immediately removes the cost advantage for the rest of the hand.

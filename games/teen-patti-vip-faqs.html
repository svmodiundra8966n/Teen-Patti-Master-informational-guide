Teen Patti VIP FAQs

Common questions about Teen Patti VIP answered simply. Always confirm live requirements inside the app.

What makes Teen Patti VIP different?

VIP usually offers private or restricted tables, often with higher entry requirements. Hand rankings and the basic round structure stay the same as Master and Gold.

Do I need a higher balance for VIP tables?

Many versions require a minimum balance or account level before VIP tables open. Check the live conditions inside the app after login.

Are the hand rankings different on VIP?

No. Trail is still the strongest hand, followed by Pure Sequence, Sequence, Colour, Pair and High Card.

Is VIP better than Gold?

They serve different purposes. Gold focuses on higher limits. VIP focuses more on restricted access. Neither is automatically better.

Should beginners play VIP?

Most beginners are better off learning on regular Teen Patti Master tables first.

Can entry requirements change?

Yes. Always check inside the app rather than relying on older information.

Is real-money VIP play legal everywhere in India?

No. Online real-money gaming is restricted under the 2025 Act. Confirm local laws. 18+ only.

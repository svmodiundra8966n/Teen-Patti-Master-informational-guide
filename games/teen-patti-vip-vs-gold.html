Teen Patti Master VIP vs Gold – Which One to Choose

VIP and Gold are both higher-stakes versions of the same three-card game. The difference is mainly in how you access the tables and the type of tables offered.

Quick Comparison

  • Gold – Usually higher limit tables that are open to more players. Focus is on bigger boot and raise amounts.
  • VIP – Often private or restricted tables. May require a higher balance or account level before the tables appear.
  • Hand Rankings – Same on both (Trail strongest, then Pure Sequence, Sequence, Colour, Pair, High Card).
  • Round Flow – Same basic structure on both.

Practical Difference

Gold is more about stake size. VIP is more about restricted access. Some players use both depending on what is available inside the app. Always check the live requirements after login because they can change.
